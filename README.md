##  Overview

404 Restaurant is a modern restaurant management and point-of-sale platform designed to streamline restaurant operations through an intuitive dashboard, intelligent order management, customer engagement tools, and real-time business analytics.

Inspired by software engineering culture, the platform introduces a unique developer-themed experience while maintaining all essential restaurant management capabilities required for real-world operations.
